TASKLOCAL POLICY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskLocal may collect, use, protect, and manage information when you use our local home-task marketplace.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

TaskLocal respects your privacy and aims to handle your information responsibly. This policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you create an account, post a task, browse professionals, communicate with users, or use other TaskLocal features.

By using TaskLocal, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, TaskLocal may collect information such as:

  • Name and account profile information.
  • Email address and contact information.
  • Login and account authentication details.
  • Task descriptions and related project information.
  • Service preferences and scheduling information.
  • Reviews, ratings, messages, and feedback.
  • Payment-related information required to process supported transactions.

3. Information You Provide

You may provide information directly when registering for an account, completing your profile, posting a task, submitting a bid, communicating with another user, contacting support, or submitting a review.

Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in public task descriptions, reviews, or messages.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and improve TaskLocal, including for purposes such as:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Connecting customers with relevant professionals.
  • Processing task requests and supported payments.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Sending important account and service notifications.
  • Improving platform features and usability.
  • Detecting suspicious activity and protecting platform security.

5. Task and Profile Information

Information included in a task listing or professional profile may be visible to other users when necessary for marketplace functionality.

Users should consider carefully what they include in public-facing descriptions, profile information, reviews, and uploaded content.

6. Communications

TaskLocal may process communications exchanged through supported platform features to provide the requested service, maintain security, assist with support requests, and address reported misuse.

Users should not use TaskLocal messaging features to share information that they do not want another participant in a task to receive.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TaskLocal may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain sessions, improve functionality, and understand how platform features are used.

Your browser may provide controls for managing cookies and related storage technologies.

8. Payment Information

Where payment functionality is available, payment information may be processed through appropriate payment services or providers.

TaskLocal may receive transaction-related information such as payment status, transaction references, or payment history without necessarily storing complete payment-card information.

9. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared when necessary to provide TaskLocal services or operate the platform.

  • With users when required to facilitate a task or marketplace interaction.
  • With service providers supporting platform operations.
  • With payment providers when required to process supported transactions.
  • When required by applicable law or lawful requests.
  • When necessary to protect the rights, security, and integrity of TaskLocal, its users, or others.

10. Information Security

TaskLocal may use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their account credentials and notify support of suspected unauthorized access.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, comply with legal requirements, and protect platform security.

12. Your Choices

Depending on the features available in your account, you may be able to review or update certain profile information.

You may also contact TaskLocal regarding questions about information associated with your account.

13. Children's Privacy

TaskLocal is intended for users who are legally permitted to use the service. The platform is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use such services.

14. Third-Party Services

TaskLocal may integrate with third-party services such as authentication providers, payment providers, analytics services, or communication tools.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

15. Policy Updates

TaskLocal may update this Privacy Policy when platform features, business practices, or applicable requirements change.

The updated version will display a revised effective or updated date. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact TaskLocal support.
